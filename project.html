7 Wonders In The World

S.No Image Description
1 The Taj Mahal is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, on the banks of the Yamuna River. It was built by the Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan between 1632 and 1653 in memory of his beloved wife, Mumtaz Mahal. Constructed entirely from white marble, it is admired for its beautiful domes, intricate carvings, and symmetrical gardens. The Taj Mahal is recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site and is one of the Seven Wonders of the World. Every year, millions of tourists from across the globe visit this iconic monument.
2 Christ the Redeemer is a giant statue of Jesus Christ located on Mount Corcovado in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. The statue stands 30 meters (98 feet) tall, with arms stretching 28 meters (92 feet) wide, symbolizing peace, love, and hope. It was completed in 1931 and is one of the most recognized landmarks in the world. Visitors from around the globe come to admire both the statue and the breathtaking view of the city..
3 The Great Wall of China is one of the most remarkable structures ever built by humans. It stretches for more than 21,000 kilometers across northern China and was constructed over many centuries to protect the country from invasions. Built using stone, brick, and earth, the wall passes through mountains, deserts, and valleys. Today, it is a symbol of China's rich history, determination, and engineering excellence, attracting millions of visitors every year.
4 Machu Picchu is an ancient Incan city located high in the Andes Mountains of Peru. Built during the 15th century, it is believed to have been a royal estate or religious center of the Inca Empire. The city is famous for its carefully crafted stone buildings, terraces, and beautiful mountain surroundings. Despite being abandoned for centuries, it remains one of the best-preserved archaeological sites and showcases the advanced engineering skills of the Inca civilization.
5 Chichén Itzá is one of the most important archaeological sites of the ancient Maya civilization in Mexico. It flourished between the 6th and 12th centuries AD and served as a major political, cultural, and religious center. The site's most famous structure is the Temple of Kukulcán (El Castillo), a pyramid designed with remarkable astronomical precision. Chichén Itzá reflects the Maya people's advanced knowledge of mathematics, architecture, and astronomy.
6 The Colosseum is a massive amphitheater located in Rome, Italy, and is one of the greatest achievements of ancient Roman architecture. Built between AD 70 and 80, it could accommodate around 50,000 spectators. It was used for gladiator contests, public performances, animal hunts, and other entertainment events. Even though parts of the structure have been damaged over time, the Colosseum remains a powerful symbol of the Roman Empire's engineering and cultural heritage.
7 Petra is an ancient city located in southern Jordan and is famous for its stunning buildings carved directly into pink sandstone cliffs. It was built by the Nabataean civilization more than 2,000 years ago and served as an important trading center connecting different regions. The most famous monument in Petra is Al-Khazneh (The Treasury), which is admired for its beautiful architecture. Petra is also known as the "Rose City" because of the color of its rocks.
